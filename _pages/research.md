<div class="research">
  <h2>Research Area</h2>

  <div class="research-area">
    <div class="research__figure research-area__figure"></div>
    <div class="research-area__text">
      <h3>1. Lithium-ion Battery Simulation</h3>
      <p>I build physics-based models of lithium-ion batteries that couple ion transport and reaction kinetics with heat generation and mechanics. The models span length scales from single particles to full cells and are used to interpret measured behavior and to identify what limits rate capability and lifetime.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="research-area">
    <div class="research__figure research-area__figure"></div>
    <div class="research-area__text">
      <h3>2. Electrode Design</h3>
      <p>I study how electrode architecture affects cell performance, including binder selection, dual-layer structures, and graphite–SiOx composite electrodes. Microstructure-resolved simulations are used to compare design options, with an emphasis on fast-charging behavior.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="research-area">
    <div class="research__figure research-area__figure"></div>
    <div class="research-area__text">
      <h3>3. Si-based All-Solid-State Battery</h3>
      <p>I investigate Si-based electrodes for all-solid-state batteries, where large volume change and solid–solid contact govern performance and degradation. This work examines how interfacial design, such as a primer layer, and electrode structure affect ion transport and mechanical stability.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <h2>Research Technology</h2>

  <div class="research-tech">
    <div class="research__figure research-tech__figure"></div>
    <h3>1. FEM Simulation</h3>
    <p>Finite element models are built in COMSOL Multiphysics at several scales: a single-particle model, multiphysics analysis of electrode microstructures, a coupled P2D–thermal model, and a lithium plating model.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-tech">
    <div class="research__figure research-tech__figure"></div>
    <h3>2. 3D Microstructure Reconstruction</h3>
    <p>Electrode microstructures are created with algorithm-based structure generation or reconstructed from tomographic data, using GeoDict and Avizo. The resulting 3D structures are characterized by their structural parameters.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-tech">
    <div class="research__figure research-tech__figure"></div>
    <h3>3. Electrode Design for LIB</h3>
    <p>Electrode design studies cover binder selection and dual-layer electrodes. Fast-charging behavior of graphite–SiOx composite electrodes is analyzed by simulation.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="research-tech">
    <div class="research__figure research-tech__figure"></div>
    <h3>4. All-Solid-State Battery (ASSB)</h3>
    <p>Design of Si-based electrodes and primer layers for all-solid-state batteries.</p>
  </div>
</div>
